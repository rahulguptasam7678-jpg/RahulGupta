How I use AI in my work

I use AI as support for marketing judgment and creative production, not a substitute for it. My approach is to start with a clear brief, refine output to the brand's voice, check every factual claim, and let campaign testing decide which creative ideas earn budget.

  • Copy and research: ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for ad copy, content ideas, strategy drafts and market research.
  • Creative testing: Google Flow for AI video variations; Canva AI and Adobe Premiere Pro for visual and edit work.
  • Search readiness: keeping client sites open to AI search crawlers so brands can be cited in AI-driven discovery.

Approach

How I work on a new engagement

01

Discover and define

Clarify the offer, audience, objective and conversion journey. Define what counts as a qualified lead or a completed purchase before choosing priorities.

02

Build and measure

Set up channels, event tracking, landing-page journeys and, for e-commerce, the checkout and payment flow, so results can be trusted.

03

Test and scale

Test audiences and creative variations, adjust bids and budgets, judge lead quality alongside cost per lead, and document what each test teaches.

Search in the AI era

Local and brand discovery now runs through AI answers as well as classic search. I treat the Google Business Profile as a landing page, keep structured data accurate, and configure robots.txt so AI crawlers can read the site.
